MY MBBS STORY

The path changed.

The road to medicine wasn't exactly straightforward. I began my university journey at RUET, Rajshahi, studying engineering — then career opportunities and my parents' dreams led me toward a different path.

I began my university journey at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Rajshahi. Engineering was an important chapter, but over time I found myself reconsidering where I wanted my career to go.

Career opportunities and my parents' dreams played an important role in that change. The direction eventually shifted toward medicine, leading to the MBBS journey I am on now.

This page will grow alongside that journey — from the curriculum and lecture notes to the resources, experiences and progress that come with medical school.
